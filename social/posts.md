## LinkedIn (pair with `nerdfest-linkedin-1200x627.png`)

This Friday (30 October), Singularity, the University of Waikato's student-led AI club, is hosting a stall at Nerd Fest 2026. We're skipping the slide deck.

Visitors talk to a voice-controlled AI agent and watch it operate a computer in real time: typing the prompt, generating the image and putting it on the TV in front of them. It's a hands-on way to see what an AI agent actually is: software that doesn't just answer you, it takes actions on your behalf. Seeing it type and click is far more convincing than being told.

A few details we're proud of:
• Kids and families can jump straight in. There's a Kid mode, and a Nerd mode that shows the prompt, models and tool calls behind the scenes.
• Every picture goes into an online gallery where visitors vote for their favourites. Voting closes at 6pm.

Nerd Fest is run by the School of Computing and Mathematical Sciences, is free, and is open to all ages. Thank you to the School for having us.
